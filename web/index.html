HOW TICKETS WORK

Ticket Setup

The ticket system lets members open private support threads. Use !ticketsetup in Discord to post the panel, or !ticketpanel to create a dropdown panel with multiple categories. Configure the destination category, the staff role that gets access, and the channel where the ticket panel button appears.

  • • Category ID — The Discord category channel ID where new ticket channels are created under.
  • • Panel Channel — The channel where the "Open a Ticket" button embed will be posted.
  • • Staff Role — This role is auto-added to every new ticket for visibility.
  • • AI Triage — When enabled, Athena's AI analyzes the ticket content and suggests the right staff to ping.
