====================================================================
 ATHENA PRIME CINEMA - LOCAL MOVIE REPOSITORY
====================================================================

Drop your downloaded movie and video files directly into this folder!
Supported video formats:
  - .mp4
  - .mkv
  - .avi
  - .mov
  - .webm
  - .flv
  - .m4v

How to stream in Discord:
  1. Join any Voice Channel in your Discord server.
  2. Type: !movie list  (to view all movies in this folder)
  3. Type: !movie play 1  (or !movie play <filename.mp4>)
  4. Athena will join your voice room, stream the high-fidelity surround
     audio, generate a mobile/web theater link, and display interactive
     control buttons (Play, Pause, Forward, Rewind, Stop).
